Hi again. I'm Tusher, the explorer.

I spend a lot of my professional life exploring networks, algorithms, and large-scale systems. Outside research, I like exploring the physical world just as much — mountains, deserts, canyons, coastlines, national parks, new countries, new roads, and trails that make the view feel earned.

I am also a sports fan and I like the fun, competitive, social side of life. This page is my growing map of the places and experiences that exist beyond the terminal.
