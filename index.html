01 · Bio

Curious by nature.
Systems researcher by profession.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Florida State University, advised by Prof. Xin Yuan. My research focuses on communication optimization in high-performance computing, especially large-scale interconnection networks.

I enjoy problems where algorithms meet real systems: adaptive routing, process mapping, congestion, allocation, simulation, and increasingly the intersection of AI and systems. Before HPC, I built AI-enabled educational simulations and worked as a software engineer.
