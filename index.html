01 · Bio

Systems researcher by profession.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Florida State University, advised by Prof. Xin Yuan. I study communication optimization and system-level performance in high-performance computing, with a particular focus on large-scale interconnection networks.

My research spans adaptive routing, process mapping, congestion analysis, node allocation, inter-job interference, and packet/MPI-level simulation. 02 · Research

What I work on — and why.

My research starts from a simple systems question: when thousands of communicating processes share a large machine, how can the network, runtime, and placement decisions work together instead of becoming bottlenecks?

01

Process Mapping & Communication-Aware Placement

I study how an application's communication structure can guide process placement on hierarchical HPC networks. The central challenge is to improve locality without simply moving congestion elsewhere in the machine.

  • Analyze the communication behavior of traditional HPC, deep-learning, and LLM workloads and how their logical communication patterns interact with the physical network.
  • Study how process placement affects network traffic, communication cost, congestion, and ultimately end-to-end application performance.
  • Investigate the tradeoff between communication locality and network load balancing when mapping applications onto large-scale hierarchical interconnection networks.
02

Adaptive Routing for Large-Scale Interconnection Networks

I study how adaptive routing can respond to congestion and changing traffic in high-radix networks such as Dragonfly. The goal is to understand how network-level routing decisions translate into application-level performance.

  • Analyze routing behavior under synthetic traffic, traditional HPC applications, and distributed deep-learning workloads.
  • Study how congestion, path choice, and traffic dynamics influence communication time and overall execution.
  • Explore both conventional and learning-based approaches to adaptive routing at a high level, with particular interest in using AI to make system decisions more responsive.
03

Communication-Aware Node Allocation

My ongoing work moves one level earlier in the execution pipeline: deciding which physical nodes a job should receive before its processes are mapped onto them. I study how application communication behavior and system fragmentation can guide this allocation decision.

  • Study how application communication behavior can inform compact-versus-spread allocation decisions.
  • Analyze fragmentation and group/router-level resource availability in large-scale systems.
  • Investigate how allocation decisions affect communication efficiency and application performance across traditional HPC and AI workloads.
04

Inter-Job Interference in Shared HPC Systems

Large-scale computing systems are shared by multiple jobs running concurrently, and their communication can interfere with one another through the network. My ongoing research studies how this inter-job interference affects application performance and how communication-aware system decisions can mitigate it.

  • Analyze how concurrently running HPC and AI workloads interact through shared network resources and how this interference affects end-to-end performance.
  • Study how intelligent routing, process mapping, and node allocation can work together to reduce cross-job contention and isolate application communication.
  • Explore interference-aware system management toward shared large-scale computing environments where multiple jobs can execute with minimal performance interference.
05

AI Workload Communication & Training Performance

I study AI workloads as communication-intensive system workloads, not as an isolated application domain. Deep-learning and LLM execution increasingly depends on how efficiently data moves among accelerators and nodes.

  • Analyze communication behavior of distributed deep-learning and LLM workloads in network simulations.
  • Study how routing and process placement can reduce communication overhead and improve execution performance for AI workloads.
  • Extend this perspective toward large-scale AI training: interconnects, collective communication, placement, routing, allocation, and contention.
06

AI for Systems

I am interested in using AI where it can make systems more adaptive, while keeping the work grounded in measurable systems objectives and strong non-AI baselines.

  • Explore learning-based routing and data-driven system decisions.
  • Study how workload, congestion, and system state can inform adaptive policies.
  • Longer term, extend AI-driven decision making toward allocation, congestion management, and other system-level optimization problems.
07

Large-Scale Interconnect Simulation & Performance Analysis

I build and use packet-level and MPI-level simulation workflows to connect network mechanisms with end-to-end workload behavior.

  • Use SST Merlin, Ember, Firefly, and BookSim for large-scale experiments.
  • Model traditional HPC motifs alongside AI/deep-learning communication patterns and collective-heavy workloads.
  • Study routing, mapping, allocation, topology variants, dynamic traffic, congestion, performance variability, and scalability.
